# Westland Stake YM Camp 2027 — "Rejoice in Christ"

Website for the Westland Michigan Stake Young Men camp: a five-day canoe trip on the Au Sable River, July 27–31, 2027.

Live site: https://westland-stake-ym.github.io/Camp-2027/

## Pages
- `index.html`: the general site for young men and families (route map, crews, packing list, safety, registration, FAQ, contacts)
- `leaders.html`: the Crew Leader Hub (private records links, checklist, cash & supply calculator, gear vehicle plan, menu, reimbursement, open items)

## Privacy
No registration, medical or permission-form data is stored on this site. The Leader Hub only **links** to a Google Sheet (responses) and a Google Drive folder (signed forms) that are shared with named leaders' Google accounts. Anyone else who clicks those links sees "Request access."

## Updating links
Open `assets/js/site.js` and fill in the `CAMP.links` settings at the top (registration form, responses sheet, forms folder, etc.). Any link left blank shows as **TBD** on the site.

## Unresolved details
Items still being decided are marked with a yellow **TBD** tag. Search the HTML for `TBD` to find them.
